Three steps, one zip at a time

Google Takeout splits a big library into many zips. Sift works through them one by one, so you never need space for all of it at once.

  1. Export

    Ask Google Takeout for Google Photos. Download the first zip to your Downloads folder.

  2. Sift it

    Open Sift, pick the zip, press Find my trips. Watch each photo get checked and fly into its folder.

  3. Keep the trips

    Your shots land in Pictures\Sift Trips\Kashmir\Srinagar and so on. Delete the zip, load the next.

What stays out

Sift is picky on purpose. Anything that isn't a travel shot is counted and left exactly where it is.

PeopleSelfies, group shots and close-up faces, found by an on-device face detector.
Around homeAnything taken within 60 km of home. You set where home is.
ReceivedWhatsApp forwards and other images that came from someone else.
ScreensScreenshots, plus shots too blurry or dark to use in a reel.
No locationNo GPS, and no nearby photo to borrow a place from.
DuplicatesTakeout copies album photos twice. Sift remembers what it kept across zips.

Fixes bad GPS

Old phones guess. Sift checks every photo against the ones taken just before and after it.

A photo 1,400 km away from both neighbours, half an hour apart, is a bad reading. Photos with no GPS borrow a place from shots taken within 3 hours.

Get Sift

  1. Download Sift.exe with the button.
  2. Double-click it. Sift asks to install itself, then adds a Desktop and Start menu shortcut.
  3. Open Sift any time from the Start menu. It opens in its own window and closes when you're done.
